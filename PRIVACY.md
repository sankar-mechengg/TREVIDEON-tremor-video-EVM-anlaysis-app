# Privacy (summary)

The full policy is on the website (Privacy page). In short:

* **Local use** (`run.ps1`): everything runs on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.
* **Hosted website**: videos, marker files and results are processed only to perform the analysis you request.
  They are kept in temporary storage under a random session id known only to your browser tab and deleted
  60 minutes after your last activity, immediately with "Delete my data", about two minutes after the tab
  is closed, and whenever the server restarts. No accounts, no analytics or tracking cookies, no sharing,
  no model training on your data, no human review.
* Upload only videos you are entitled to process (your own, or with the informed consent of the person
  recorded and, for research, ethics approval).

This summary and the website policy are templates for the research tool and should be reviewed by
IIT Delhi before public launch.
